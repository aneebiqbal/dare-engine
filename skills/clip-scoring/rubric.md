# Viral moment rubric

Score every dimension **0–10**. The composite is the weighted sum × 10 (0–100).

| Dimension | Weight | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hook_strength` | 0.25 | Do the first ~3 seconds make a viewer stop scrolling? Bold claim, question, number, contradiction, visible reaction. |
| `emotional_peak` | 0.20 | Is there surprise, humor, anger, hype or a genuine reaction — not flat narration? |
| `payoff_delivery` | 0.20 | Does the window resolve something inside itself, with no "watch the full video" energy? |
| `standalone_clarity` | 0.15 | Can a viewer state what this is about in one sentence without any prior context? |
| `pace_energy` | 0.10 | Does it move, or is there dead air / filler / a long warm-up? |
| `quotability` | 0.05 | Is there a line worth quoting or sharing on its own? |
| `length_fit` | 0.05 | Is the length right for the idea (14–45 s sweet spot; never padded with a second weaker idea)? |

## Bands — use the whole range

Do not cluster at 8–9. These anchors exist to spread scores.

- **9–10** — Exceptional. A 9 hook is a line you would screenshot; a 9 payoff
  lands like a punchline; a 9 standalone clip needs nothing else.
- **7–8** — Strong. Clearly worth posting, minor dead air or a softer close.
- **5–6** — Usable. A real hook or a real payoff, but not both; the other is
  merely adequate.
- **3–4** — Weak. Flat delivery, vague point, or a fragment that leans on
  missing context.
- **0–2** — Reject territory. No hook, no payoff, or incoherent/fragment.

Worked examples:

- Transcript: *"I lost $240,000 in one weekend. Everyone said I was done — then
  I found the one mistake that actually saved me."* → hook 9, emotional 8,
  payoff 5 (not yet delivered), standalone 8, pace 7, quotable 9, length 8.
  Composite ≈ 76. Selected.
- Transcript: *"So anyway we kept playing and it was pretty good I guess, and
  then later we did another thing."* → hook 2, emotional 2, payoff 1,
  standalone 3, pace 3, quotable 1, length 5. Composite ≈ 24. Rejected.
- Transcript: *"People think the boss is unbeatable. Here's the exact route I
  used — and the third attempt is where it goes wrong."* → hook 8, emotional 7,
  payoff 8, standalone 7, pace 8, quotable 7, length 8. Composite ≈ 76.
  Selected.

## Kill criteria (hard rejects)

A candidate is rejected regardless of its composite when:

- `hook_strength` < 4 (weak or missing hook), or
- `payoff_delivery` < 4 (nothing resolves inside the window), or
- the window starts mid-thought, on a filler ban ("so anyway", "and then"), on a
  greeting/sponsor/CTA, or depends on unseen context.

## Labels (pick exactly one per clip)

`funny`, `surprising`, `spicy` (controversial/bold claim), `insight`,
`reaction`, `story`, `tip`, `quote`.

The final set should mix labels; do not let one label dominate a single video.

## Hook lines

The hook line is burned on screen over the first seconds, so it must be **4–10
words**, punchy and verbatim from the transcript where possible. Never a whole
sentence, never a summary longer than ten words. If the strongest line is long,
cut it to its first clause that still lands.
