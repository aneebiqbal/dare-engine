# Clipdesk guide

## How do I accept a mission?
Open **Today**, read the Daily Mission and your picks, then choose **Accept**.
Accepted missions appear on the **Remote** page.

## How do I record a mission?
Open **Remote** on your phone. Tap **Start** when the mission begins and **Done**
when it ends, then enter the result. If you lose connection, taps are queued and
sent with their original timestamps when you are back online.

## How do I upload a recording instead of streaming?
Open **Clips → Upload**, choose the recording, add one entry per mission with a
start and end time, then upload. The file is uploaded in chunks, so a dropped
connection resumes instead of restarting.

## How do I turn a mission into a Short?
Each Start/Done pair creates one render job. Watch the stage (fetch, transcribe,
select, render, store) on the **Clips** page. When it is ready, open it to
retitle, trim, pick a layout and caption style, then **Approve**.

## How do I add a voice-over?
Open a clip, turn on **Voice-over**, pick a voice and preview it. The hook line
and an outro line are spoken over the gameplay, which ducks underneath. For
monetisation, your own voice is the safer choice.

## How do I export a Short?
Open a clip and choose **Export package**. You get the video, a thumbnail, a
title, a description and tags in one download. Publishing to YouTube is not
part of this build.

## How do I see how my missions performed?
Open **Results**. Enter views and average view percentage per clip, or connect a
collector later. The AI summary names the mission types that are working and
cites the numbers.

## How do I invite a creator?
Admins open **Invites**, create a code, and give it to the creator. Sign-up is
invite-only. Admins can also issue a password reset link from **Users**.
